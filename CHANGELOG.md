# Changelog

## Unreleased

## 0.2.3 – 2026-11-01

- **The warm finish is called Linen** (it was Spool) in Settings. It looks the same, and a saved "Spool" choice opens as Linen. From TAW-Panel 0.5.0.

## 0.2.2 – 2026-11-01

- **One Analyser at a time.** Clicking the Analyser in another app's family switcher (or opening the Analyser again) brings the open one to the front, restored if it was minimised, instead of starting a second Analyser.
- Starting another family app no longer leaves a finished process behind each time.

## 0.2.1 – 2026-10-16

Lined up with the Player and Equaliser.

- The family's tighter look (TAW-Panel 0.4.0), as in the Player and Equaliser: the nameplate is the badge and model line without the big ANALYSER, the top band is shorter (the display gets the room), corners are tighter, and the chosen half of Simple / Detailed stands out.
- **The window resizes from its corners** too, and every time (TAW-Panel 0.3.2).
- **The spectrum falls away when the sound stops.** On Windows nothing comes through loopback while the output is silent, so the spectrum stood still where the music ended; after a quarter of a second with no audio it now drops to the floor, and the peak marks follow.

## 0.2.0 – 2026-10-14

The new TAW look, from TAW-Panel 0.3.0, matching the Equaliser.

- Spool's look by default (Light, Amber), with Spool's seven colour themes tinting the whole panel, and Porcelain as the alternative; each in Light, Dark or Automatic (following the computer). Chosen in Settings (the gear).
- The display window fills the window; the controls sit in a bar along the bottom. The badge, logo and model line stay as before.
- The Analyser draws its own title strip: drag it to move the window, double-click to maximise, and use its own minimise, maximise and close buttons. Settings has "Use the system title bar" to bring back the system's.
- Simple view: just the source, Smooth/Bars/LEDs, Freeze, Stay on top and Full screen. Detailed has everything.
- The family switcher starts the Library, Player or Equaliser when they're installed, and says so along the top of the display when one isn't.
- The spectrum draws in the display's own ink by default (white, or dark on light Porcelain); the colour schemes are still in the Colour menu.

## 0.1.0 – 2026-10-13

The SP-10 Spectrum Analyser, moved out of EQ Box into its own program and renamed the Analyser (SP-001).

- Same features as SP-10 in EQ Box 0.2.0: outputs and inputs on PipeWire and WASAPI, curve, bars or LEDs at 1/3, 1/6 or 1/12 octave, seven colour schemes, rise, fall and hold, range and slope, full screen, always on top and freeze.
- Its own icon. Settings start fresh (SP-10's aren't carried over).
